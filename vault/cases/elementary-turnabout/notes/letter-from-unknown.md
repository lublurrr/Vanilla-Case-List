There was a second letter found on the bureau of the victim in the classroom. It was ripped in two and the bottom half is missing. It is unknown who the sender is, since it was written using a typing machine. It reads as follows:

> Adrian! Why would you do this to me! How could you.. was our happiness not good enough for you?! I never expected you to betray me and-*ripped and unreadable*- I even found that- *ripped and unreadable*- on your desk today!-*ripped and unreadable*

Addressed to [[adrian-andrews|Adrian Andrews]]. Unlike the [[letter-from-the-defendant|Letter from the defendant]], this one is typed rather than handwritten.
