![Map of the school at the moment the crime scene was found](icons/crime-scene-map.png)

This is at the moment [[kristoph-gavin|Kristoph Gavin]] stumbled upon the crime scene. It was drawn by [[ema-skye|Ema Skye]].

## Reading the markers

- **V** is [[adrian-andrews|Adrian Andrews]], in the corridor at the north end of the third grade classroom.
- **D** is [[ini-miney|Ini Miney]], directly beside the victim.
- **W** markers are the two witnesses. One stands north west, outside near the fourth grade classroom end of the building. The other stands inside, at the west side of the gym.

## Layout

The gym sits at the centre of the building. The fourth grade classroom is to the north, the Directors Office to the east, the Kitchen to the south east, the fifth grade classroom to the south, and the third grade classroom to the west.

## Windows

Windows are not drawn on the map, but every classroom has a huge window that reaches all the way along the inside wall next to the doors. This applies to every classroom on the map.
