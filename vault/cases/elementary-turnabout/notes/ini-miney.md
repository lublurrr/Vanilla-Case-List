**Occupation:** Fifth grade Teacher

The main suspect of this case. Holds a grudge against the victim.

She was found at the scene next to [[adrian-andrews|Adrian Andrews]], marked **D** on the [[crime-scene-map|Crime Scene Map]], at the moment [[kristoph-gavin|Kristoph Gavin]] arrived.

The [[knife|Knife]] recovered from the victim's chest is hers and carries only her fingerprints. Her handwriting appears on the [[letter-from-the-defendant|Letter from the defendant]], and the [[bottle-of-pills|Bottle of Pills]] bears her prints as well.
