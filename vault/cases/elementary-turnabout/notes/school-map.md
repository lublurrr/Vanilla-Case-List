A map of the school, created based on eyewitness descriptions at the time the victim was attacked. The defendant and the witnesses could have been at different places, since they weren't proven to be there. The victim was attacked and found at the marked spot. Every classroom has a huge window on the inside next to the doors.

The map itself is the [[crime-scene-map|Crime Scene Map]]. It was drawn by [[ema-skye|Ema Skye]].
