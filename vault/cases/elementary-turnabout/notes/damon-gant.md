**Occupation:** Fourth grade Teacher

Gant is one of the teachers working at the school. He is kind to his pupils and is the current fourth grade teacher. He claims to have been watchin over the children during breaktime outside and therefore doesn't have much information about the crime.

One of the two **W** markers on the [[crime-scene-map|Crime Scene Map]] is his position at the time of the attack.
